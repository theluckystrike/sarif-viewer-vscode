# Problems panel and compare

- "Show in Problems" adds the results (with your filters) to the Problems panel. The rule code links to its help page. Click again, or close the log, and they are removed.
- "Compare…" asks for an older log of the same project. Results are matched by their fingerprints and marked new, unchanged or fixed.
- "Add log…" shows a second log in the same table, with a tool column.
